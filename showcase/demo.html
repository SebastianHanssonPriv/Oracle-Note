Build notes

What's real here

  • Every screen, transition and field is the actual copy, layout and Bufab styling from the working React Native build.
  • The spoken lines use your browser's built-in text-to-speech (Web Speech API), triggered on tap — no audio files, nothing recorded.

What's simulated

  • There's no real voice recognition here. The dashed controls under the car display stand in for the spoken commands the app itself listens for.
  • Choosing "Later" here ends the demo session (Inactive). In the real app that same choice persists progress on-device and resumes from Home — this demo doesn't model multi-visit state, so that part isn't shown.
  • One fixed demo visit (Bergman Maskin AB), same as the full build and the walkthrough.
