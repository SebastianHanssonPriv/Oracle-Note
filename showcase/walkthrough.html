How the flow works

One spoken debrief, staged and confirmed before it ever touches the CRM.

A rep finishes a customer visit, gets in the car, and talks for one to two minutes. Oracle Note transcribes it, matches the customer from the calendar, extracts what it can, and asks about the rest once the car has stopped. Nothing reaches the CRM until the rep confirms it on the phone.

The brief

Who it's for

A C-parts trading rep, fasteners and consumables, kanban bins and frame agreements. The sample content throughout is one real-shaped visit: an existing customer, a proposal in progress, a competitor undercutting on price.

What was settled

  • The car screen carries state only: elapsed time and one big target.
  • Monologue first; Oracle then asks, right there in the car, whether to continue with the gap questions now or later.
  • The customer is matched from calendar, timestamp and location, not guessed from the transcript.
  • Uncertainty is shown as a question, never as a confidence score.
  • Every staged field keeps a visible origin: extracted, or something the rep said.

What this build is

A working React Native app (iOS and Android, one codebase) implementing this exact flow against a fixed demo visit. Voice capture, transcription, calendar matching and the CRM push are not wired to real services yet; see the build notes at the end of this page.

Why it looks like this

The first version of this screen made the rep wait until the car had parked before it would ask anything — a rule enforced by the app rather than a choice the rep made. That's gone: the moment the monologue ends, Oracle asks directly, still by voice, still nothing to tap. Build notes

What's real in this build

  • The full interaction flow, all eight screens, wired end to end in React Native for iOS and Android.
  • The visual system above: paper ground, blueprint registration marks, Barlow / Barlow Condensed, carried over pixel-for-pixel from the design review.
  • The demo dataset: a single fixed visit (Bergman Maskin AB) with real-shaped fields, questions and transcript text.

Not wired up yet

  • Real speech-to-text, calendar matching and location, and a live CRM push. These screens run on the fixed demo dataset, not a backend.
  • An actual CarPlay / Android Auto surface. Screens 02–04 render inside the phone app as a non-interactive, landscape-framed card; a real in-car surface needs a separate native extension.
  • Two of the three spoken gap questions (next step, consumption change) use written-for-demo copy; only the decision-maker question was scripted in the original design.
  • Choosing "later" on step 04 ends the session outright (Inactive) rather than partially staging it; there's no save-and-resume layer yet, so that visit doesn't actually come back later in this build.
