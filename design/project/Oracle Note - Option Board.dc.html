00Oracle Note — brief & assumptions
What it is

A rep finishes a customer visit, gets in the car, and talks for 1–2 minutes. The app transcribes, extracts CRM fields, then asks about the gaps it could not resolve. Nothing is written to the CRM until the rep confirms on the phone.

Sample content throughout is a C-parts trading rep — fasteners and consumables, kanban bins, frame agreements.

Settled by your answers
  • Car screen carries state only: elapsed time and one big target.
  • Monologue first, then gap questions — who decides & who signs, next step + date.
  • The customer is matched automatically from calendar, timestamp and location — no account guess to confirm during capture.
  • Gap questions are held until the car has stopped; the car display never asks anything.
  • Uncertainty is shown as a question, never as a low-confidence value.
  • Tapping a field opens a transcript sheet with its source line.
  • Steel-on-paper wireframe, light ground, in the car too.
My assumptions
  • Your repo is empty, so the visual language comes entirely from the Industry design system.
  • "Your CRM" is generic — one push action, seven fields, no vendor chrome.
  • The car display is a landscape 560×320-ish surface; the phone screens are 340-wide.
  • Recording keeps running if the phone locks or the car disconnects.
